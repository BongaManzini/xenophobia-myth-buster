# 🇿🇦 Xenophobia Myth-Buster: Real Stats vs Social Media Rumors
### A Student Research Project on South African Migration & Census 2022 Data

> **Live Website**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/xenophobia-myth-buster/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/xenophobia-myth-buster/)  
> **Built by**: Bonga Manzini (BSc Informatics student at UNISA)

---

## 🔬 About This Project

I built this interactive website because I got tired of seeing wild rumors on social media claiming that 20 to 30 million immigrants live in South Africa taking all the jobs and social grants.

As a student studying informatics and data, I wanted to find out what the real official data says. I downloaded the official Stats SA Census 2022 report and built this simple interactive quiz so anyone can test their assumptions against real numbers.

---

## The 11 Questions: The Facts Behind The Project

### 1. Who is this for?
- Everyday South Africans, students, teachers, and community members who want honest facts rather than social media rumors.

### 2. Where does this focus on?
- South Africa, especially busy cities like Johannesburg, Pretoria, and Cape Town where tensions and protests often flare up.

### 3. Why did I build it?
- Whenever youth unemployment gets high, it is easy for politicians and online influencers to blame outsiders. When people see the real, verified numbers from our official Census, it helps cool down anger and allows us to focus on real economic solutions.

### 4. How does the website work?
- You first move a slider to guess what percentage of people in South Africa are immigrants.
- Once you make your guess, it reveals what the official Census found.
- It also shows data on how immigrant-owned township shops actually hire local South African workers.

### 5. When did these events happen?
- From 1994 to today. It is based on the latest **Stats SA Census 2022** report released in October 2023 and updated for 2026.

### 6. The Problem I Noticed
- **The Viral Myth**: Posts on Twitter/X, TikTok, and WhatsApp claim that between 15 million and 30 million foreigners live in South Africa and receive SASSA grants.
- **The Reality**: According to the official Stats SA Census 2022, there are **2,422,968 foreign-born residents in South Africa (about 3.9% of our 62 million population)**. This matches the world average of 3.6%. Also, non-citizens cannot legally receive SASSA child or pension grants.

### 7. Where I Got The Data
1. **Stats SA Census 2022**: Official Statistical Release P0301.4 from Statistics South Africa.
2. **OECD & International Labour Organization (ILO)**: Joint study on how immigrant businesses contribute to South Africa's economy.
3. **Department of Home Affairs (DHA)**: White Paper on Citizenship and Immigration (Gazette 49661).

### 8. Why I Trusted This Data
- Stats SA is South Africa's official constitutional statistics agency under the Statistics Act of 1999.
- The OECD/ILO report conducted real, on-the-ground surveys in townships instead of guessing.

### 9. How I Built It & Cleaned The Data
- I extracted the tables from the government PDF and Excel sheets.
- I cleaned the numbers into simple JSON files.
- I built the website using HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Chart.js so it opens quickly on mobile phones with low data.

### 10. What I Hope To Achieve
- Give my friends, family, and community a fast link to check facts whenever fake numbers start spreading on WhatsApp or social media.

### 11. Why This Matters In Real Life
- Misinformation fuels violence and fear. Having quick access to verified facts protects communities and encourages healthy, evidence-based conversations.
