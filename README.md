# 🇿🇦 Xenophobia Myth-Buster: Demographic Reality Check

> **Live Application**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/xenophobia-myth-buster/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/xenophobia-myth-buster/)

An interactive data visualization tool synthesizing official **Statistics South Africa (Census 2022)**, Department of Home Affairs (DHA), and OECD/World Bank research to counter demographic misinformation and xenophobic narratives in South Africa.

---

## 🎯 The Informatics & Civic Verification Angle

- **The Problem**: Populist political campaigns and viral social media posts claim that *"foreigners make up 30% to 50% of the South African population"* or that *"foreigners have taken all the jobs."*
- **The Informatics Principle**: *"Data shrinks the myth."* When citizens are invited to test their perception against empirical census enumeration, the psychological inflation driving hostility collapses.
- **The Empirical Reality**:
  - Total Enumerated Population: **62,027,503**
  - Total Foreign-Born Residents: **2,422,968 (3.9%)**
  - South African Born Citizens: **59,604,535 (96.1%)**
  - Global average immigrant population share (UN DESA): **3.6%** (South Africa is completely consistent with international norms).

---

## 📊 Economic & Job Creation Evidence

- **Job Multiplier**: OECD (2018) research found that immigrant-owned township enterprises employ an average of **2.3 South African citizens** each.
- **Fiscal Contribution**: Net GDP contribution of immigrant workers is positive (+1.9% of GDP per capita).
- **Social Grants**: Undocumented and temporary foreign nationals are completely excluded from SASSA social grants (verified via Home Affairs National Population Register).

---

## 🛠️ Features

- **Interactive Perception Slider**: Users guess the immigrant percentage, followed by an animated reveal contrasting their estimate with Census 2022.
- **Country of Origin Distribution**: Visual breakdown of SADC and international immigrant origins (Zimbabwe 45.5%, Mozambique 18.7%, Lesotho 10.2%, Malawi 6.8%).
- **Economic Multiplier Metrics**: Data cards summarizing job creation and GDP impact.
