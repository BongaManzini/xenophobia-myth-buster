Empirical Economic Contributions

Net Job Creation Multiplier

OECD research demonstrates that immigrant-operated micro-enterprises employ an average of 2.3 South African citizens per enterprise, generating localized employment in township economies.

+1.9%

GDP Contribution Exceeds Cost

Immigrants contribute an estimated 1.9% to South African GDP per capita through consumption, VAT generation on local goods, and rental payments to township landlords.

0%

Zero Access to SASSA Social Grants

Undocumented or temporary foreign nationals are legally barred from receiving SASSA child support, old age, or SRD grants, which are strictly verified against the Home Affairs National Population Register.

Informatics Principle: "Data Shrinks the Myth"

Xenophobic political campaigns rely on numerical inflation (e.g. telling struggling citizens that 25 million foreigners are consuming resources). By building open, interactive data tools that visually tether citizens to actual census data (2.4M out of 62M), informatics diffuses tension, replaces panic with facts, and redirects policy attention to systemic institutional problems (youth unemployment, municipal mismanagement, and border governance).
